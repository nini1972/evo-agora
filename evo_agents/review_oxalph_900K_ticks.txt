## 3. Finding A — κ=0.50 flat is the decay-to-prior fix working, and the metric going blind

A perfectly flat κ=0.50 across five screenshots spanning 4k ticks is not cooperation at 50% — it's the EMA decayed to its neutral prior because **trade/raid events are rare**. The fix I recommended (decay toward prior during event-poverty) was implemented and now does its job too well: κ no longer tells you anything. The histogram's invisible green bars say trade volume is near zero.

But note — **thin trade at EHI≈0.3 is correct economics, not a bug.** When everyone is food-stressed, everyone's `k_v` is exponentially inflated, everyone's ρ is high, nobody has a food surplus worth selling, and the double coincidence of surpluses vanishes. Famine freezes food markets; ore is unfed hunger ("geld kan honger niet kopen"). The price machinery is *behaving*; it just has nothing to trade. **Metric repair:** add a rolling `handels_per_100_tik` count to `TikSteekproef` so volume is measured directly instead of inferred from a decayed EMA.

## 4. Finding B — the society has found a Malthusian subsistence attractor

EHI sits at ~0.3 and has stopped falling *and* stopped rising. My analysis of why:

- Consumption ≈ regrowth at R≈470. Any resource surplus is converted into population (the EHI-scaled target), any deficit kills agents — so R is pinned where yield = consumption. This is the classic **Malthusian trap**, and credit where due: the governor extension turned the old overshoot-collapse cycle (EHI 0.22, N crashing to 20) into a smooth, *higher* equilibrium (EHI ~0.31). It stabilized the trap rather than causing it.
- **The rich-starve pathology persists**: screenshot 4's table shows `DOOD_HONGER` with omvang 29 and 35 — agents dying of hunger with a fortune in ore. The liquidity trap I flagged last time was never fixed (scraping was P3). In famine, ore buys nothing.
- And I found a **policy weakness that feeds it**: in `kies_actie`, the BEWEEG utility is a *constant* — `beste_buurcel` picks a value-informed direction, but the action's score doesn't scale with how good the target is. A starving agent next to nothing wanders with the same drive as one next to a rich food node. Cheap, high-impact patch:

```python
# policy.kies_actie — vervang de constante BEWEEG-score door waarde-gevoelige score:
cel = perceptie.beste_buurcel(self.waarde)
if cel is not None:
    wx, wy = a.wereld.breedte, a.wereld.hoogte
    bron = a.wereld.grid.get(((a.pos[0] + cel[0]) % wx, (a.pos[1] + cel[1]) % wy))
    score = 0.35 if bron is None else (
        0.35 + 1.9 * min(1.0, self.waarde(bron.res, min(bron.hoeveelheid, 8.0)) / 45.0))
    kandidaten.append((score * (1.0 + 0.3 * g.waarde("nieuwsgierigheid")),
                       Actie(ActieType.BEWEEG, richting=cel)))
```

Hongerige agenten lopen nu richting waarde in plaats van dood te sterven naast een voedsel-node die ze één cel te ver zaten.

## 5. Finding C — genetic stasis: the peace is institutional, not genetic

This is the most important scientific observation in the 900k run. The genfrequencies panel is a **neutral tangle around 0.5 for 1200+ generations** — no directional drift, not even for `stofwisseling`, which *should* be under relentless selection (lower metabolism = lower cost *and* higher max energy; m→0 is strictly fitter). Flat best-fitness is expected in an EHI-capped world, but neutral gene-means are not.

Diagnosis: **selection pressure < mutation + drift.** At N≈40 with high environmental variance (position luck dominates fitness), the gene→fitness signal is swamped. The consequence is profound: *the pacification you're seeing is being enforced by institutions (Kroniek, raid-tax, retaliation, ostracism) on a genome that never assimilated cooperation.* The parasites aren't extinct — they're suppressed. Which yields the key experiment:

> **Ablation: Kroniek off.** ≥10 seeds, short runs, everything else identical. Prediction if Finding C is right: P rebounds to 0.3+ within ~10 generations. If it doesn't, the genes did assimilate and the tangle misled us. Either result is publishable; right now we don't know which world we're running.

And the audit that tells us whether selection is operating at all: Mann-Kendall trend per gene over generation-*blocked* means (block-average the JSONL `gen_gem` per `generatie` first — the sawtooth autocorrelation will otherwise fake significance). With 9 genes, expect 0–1 false positives at α=0.05 under pure drift. If all flat → raise the selection-to-drift ratio (larger N, lower σ-floor, or milder macro-mutation) or consciously accept that this is a drift regime.
